# Internal Refund Policy (v3) — INTERNAL USE ONLY

Refunds are issued only after invoice ownership and payment status are verified.
High-value refunds (>= $200) require human approval before the money moves.

## Evidence checklist

- Invoice exists and is marked paid.
- The ticket describes a genuine billing error (e.g. a duplicate charge).
- The requester is the invoice owner.
